A — Toggle chevron

A small chevron (↓/↑) at the right of the pill toggles expansion. Collapsed = current compact pill. Expanded = grows down to show notes text + subtask checkboxes parsed from markdown lines (- [ ] task). Clean, minimal.

B — Click title, detached card

Click the task title to open a detached notes card that floats below the pill (separate element). The pill itself stays compact. Notes card can be dismissed by clicking elsewhere. Good for keeping the pill small.

Subtask format

Both options parse subtask checkboxes from the existing notes field using markdown syntax — no separate field needed. Lines starting with - [ ] become unchecked, - [x] become checked. Toggling a checkbox updates the notes text in place.
